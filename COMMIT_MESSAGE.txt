Landing page: lead with the track, drop the preamble, widen the supply flow

Four changes to Browse the atlas, all of them about getting the figures in
front of the reader sooner and making the flow chart legible.

Removed the coverage note above the dashboard. It opened the landing page with
a paragraph of caveats -- modelled region, partial nef coverage, supply versus
observed frequency -- between the reader and the atlas. All of it is on the
Reference and Download tab, which is where the portal's fine print lives. The
nef case still explains itself where it matters: a query for a nef position
above 72 returns the full explanation in place of a result, which is the moment
a reader actually needs it.

Removed the "Pick a position" paragraph. It described the controls that sit
beside it and the figures directly below it.

The track now comes before its own controls. It is both the headline figure and
the primary way to pick a position, so a reader reaching the Position dashboard
heading meets the plot rather than a row of selects; smoothing window and supply
class refine what is already on screen.

Removed the quantity table under the track on this page. It restated numbers the
caption and the dashboard below already carry. drawSupplyTrack keeps its statsEl
option and "Your sequence" still renders one, where the values are the reader's
own and appear nowhere else on that page.

The supply-flow chart is now full width, stacked under the pie, and its geometry
scales. The chart reserves label gutters of 320 px on the left and 290 on the
right before drawing a single band; those were fixed, so at the half-width slot
the dashboard gave it the two gutters overran each other and the bands came out
with negative width. The chart drew its two columns and nothing between them --
the flows were simply not there. Two fixes, because either alone leaves a trap:
the gutters now scale with the figure and yield before the bands do, holding a
180 px minimum band span at any width; and the chart takes the full figure width
on the dashboard, which is the width it has on the batch tab, so the same chart
reads the same way on both. Bands span 498 px there, against 0 before.

test/browse_flow_cases.mjs pins the band span at 1140, 900, 700, 600, 480, 360
and 300 px, since a silently invisible band is exactly the failure that shipped.
The panel checks now assert the stacked layout and the caption's Above/Below
wording.

Service worker cache v7 -> v8: index.html changed, and without a bump a
returning reader keeps the previous page from cache.

All 12 suites and the integrity check pass.
