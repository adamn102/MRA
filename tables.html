MRA Table

Mutation rates for documented resistance mutations and broadly neutralizing antibody escape positions, one table per class, followed by a custom query for your own list. The rate is mutational supply — how often the variant is generated per replication — not how often it is seen clinically. Selection acts on top of it. Both are shown.

Each row carries its own codon decomposition; see Reference and Download for how to read the codon-options and codon-sensitivity columns, and for the rest of the portal's fine print.
