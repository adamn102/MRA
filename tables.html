MRA Table

Mutation rates for documented resistance mutations and broadly neutralizing antibody escape positions, one table per class, followed by a custom query for your own list.

These mutation rates are for HIV-1 NL4-3. Each rate is the supply of that change from the NL4-3 codon and its sequence context. If the sequence you are interested in does not share that codon or context, the rate can differ, and it may be better to create the table on Your sequence.
